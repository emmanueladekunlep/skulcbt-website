FAQ

Agent Questions

How do I get paid?+

Bank transfer monthly. Minimum withdrawal is ₦10,000.

Do I need experience?+

No. We provide the pitch, the demo, and the marketing materials. All you need is a phone and the drive to visit schools.

Is there a registration fee?+

Yes, ₦10,000 paid once. Lifetime access, no renewals.

What products do I sell?+

SkulCBT licenses: Essential (₦20k termly), Professional (₦40k termly), Enterprise (₦75k termly). Yearly and lifetime plans are also available.

How do I get started?+

Register on the agent portal, get your referral code, and start sharing with schools. Your account is live immediately.

What does my downline actually earn me?+

Every sale your recruits make pays you 5% (Level 1). If they recruit agents, you earn 3% (Level 2) and 1% (Level 3) on those deeper sales too.
