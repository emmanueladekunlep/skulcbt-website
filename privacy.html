## 1.3 Definitions

Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.

Data Subject means the person to whom personal data relates.

Processing means any operation performed on personal data, including collection, storage, use, disclosure, and deletion.

Data Controller means the person or entity that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data.

Data Processor means a person or entity that processes personal data on behalf of a Data Controller.

Consent means freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous agreement to the processing of personal data.

Sensitive Personal Data means data relating to race, ethnicity, religion, health, sexual orientation, political opinions, biometric data, and other categories defined as sensitive under the NDPA.

## 1.5 How We Collect Personal Data

We collect personal data in the following ways:

  • Directly from you — when you fill a form, sign a contract, or contact us
  • From schools — who upload data on behalf of their students, staff, and parents
  • Automatically — through cookies and analytics when you visit our website
  • From third parties — such as the Corporate Affairs Commission or credit reference agencies (for Agents)

## 1.6 Why We Process Personal Data (Lawful Basis)

We process personal data for the following purposes and lawful bases:

1. To provide SkulCBT services to schools — lawful basis: Contract

2. To process parent and student data on behalf of schools — lawful basis: Contract (we are a Data Processor; the school is the Data Controller)

3. To manage the Parent Portal — lawful basis: Consent

4. To send newsletters and updates — lawful basis: Consent (you may unsubscribe at any time)

5. To comply with Nigerian law (tax, NDPA, employment) — lawful basis: Legal Obligation

6. To manage our relationship with Agents and Partners — lawful basis: Contract

7. To improve our products and services — lawful basis: Legitimate Interests

8. To protect against fraud and security threats — lawful basis: Legitimate Interests

9. To process job applications — lawful basis: Consent and Legitimate Interests

We do not process personal data for any purpose other than those listed above.

We share personal data only in the following circumstances:

1. With the School (where the School is the Data Controller)

  • When we process data on behalf of a School, the School is the Data Controller. We act only on the School's instructions.

2. With Service Providers (Data Processors)

We use the following third-party services:

  • Cloudflare — for cloud hosting, databases, and the Parent Portal
  • PythonAnywhere — for the agent system and license management
  • Vercel — for the marketing website
  • Google Analytics — for website analytics
  • Facebook Pixel — for advertising measurement
  • Banking partners — for processing payments

Each of these providers is bound by a written Data Processing Agreement and provides adequate security.

3. With Regulators (when required by law)

  • Nigeria Data Protection Commission
  • Federal Inland Revenue Service
  • Other regulators as required by Nigerian law

4. With Law Enforcement (when required by law)

  • Nigerian Police and other law enforcement agencies

We do NOT:

  • Sell, rent, or trade personal data
  • Share personal data for marketing purposes
  • Share personal data with data brokers or advertisers
  • Transfer personal data outside Nigeria without adequate safeguards

## 1.9 Your Rights Under the NDPA 2023

You have the following rights over your personal data:

1. Right to Access — You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

2. Right to Correction — You may request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

3. Right to Deletion — You may request that we delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements).

4. Right to Restrict Processing — You may request that we restrict how we process your data.

5. Right to Object — You may object to certain processing (e.g., direct marketing).

6. Right to Data Portability — You may request a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.

7. Right to Withdraw Consent — Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time.

8. Right to Complain — You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at emmanueladekunlep@gmail.com.

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

  • Measure website traffic
  • Improve our services

Types of cookies we use:

## 1.11 Children's Data

SkulCBT processes data of children (students) on behalf of schools.

We do:

  • Process children's data only as directed by the school
  • Apply additional security measures to children's data
  • Comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the Child Rights Act 2003

We do not:

  • Use children's data for marketing
  • Profile children
  • Make automated decisions about children
  • Share children's data with third parties for commercial purposes

Schools are responsible for obtaining consent from parents and guardians for the processing of student data.

## 1.12 Security

We take security seriously. We use the following measures to protect personal data:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS, TLS)
  • Encryption at rest (where technically feasible)
  • Password hashing (PBKDF2 for Parent PINs)
  • Two-factor authentication for internal systems
  • Access controls (least privilege)
  • Logging and monitoring
  • Regular security testing
  • Employee training
  • Incident response procedures
  • Business continuity and disaster recovery

Despite these measures, no system is completely secure. If you become aware of any security concern, contact us immediately.

## 1.13 Data Breach Notification

If a personal data breach occurs, we will:

  • Notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours
  • Notify affected individuals without undue delay (if the breach is high risk)
  • Notify affected schools within 24 hours
  • Take all reasonable steps to contain and remediate the breach

You may find our full Data Breach Response Plan in our Terms of Service.

## 1.14 International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (Cloudflare, Vercel, Google) may process personal data outside Nigeria.

Where this occurs:

  • We ensure the recipient country has adequate data protection laws, OR
  • We put in place appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses), OR
  • We obtain your explicit consent

All transfers comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
