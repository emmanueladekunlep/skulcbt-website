# THE SKULCBT REFUND POLICY

## Public-Facing Policy for All Customers

# CHAPTER 1

# REFUND POLICY

## 1.1 Introduction

This Refund Policy explains when and how customers of SkulCBT may obtain a refund for licence fees paid to Labelreach Advertising Ltd ("the Company").

This Policy forms part of the SkulCBT Terms of Service and should be read together with the Customer Agreement and the Privacy Policy.

By purchasing a SkulCBT licence, the Customer agrees to this Refund Policy.

## 1.2 Our Commitment

We want every customer to be satisfied with SkulCBT. We stand behind our product. But we also believe that fairness works both ways. This Policy is designed to be fair to the customer and to the Company.

If you are not satisfied with SkulCBT, please contact us first. We will do our best to resolve the issue before considering a refund.

## 1.3 Refund Windows

The following refund windows apply to all SkulCBT licences:

Within 14 days of activation:

  • Full refund of the licence fee.
  • Less any payment processing charges incurred by the Company (typically bank charges).
  • No questions asked.

Between 15 and 30 days of activation:

  • 50% refund of the licence fee.
  • The remaining 50% is retained by the Company to cover onboarding, training, and setup costs already incurred.

After 30 days of activation:

  • No refund is available.
  • The licence remains active for its full term.

The "activation date" is the date the Customer first activates the licence using a valid licence key.

## 1.4 Refund on Lifetime Licences

Lifetime Licences are subject to the same windows as Termly and Yearly licences:

  • Within 14 days of activation: Full refund.
  • Between 15 and 30 days: 50% refund.
  • After 30 days: No refund.

However, no refund shall be granted for the difference between a Lifetime licence and a lower-tier licence (e.g., if the customer paid part of a Lifetime fee and the licence was later converted to a Yearly licence under Clause 1.6 of the Customer Agreement).

## 1.5 No Refund Situations

No refund shall be granted in the following circumstances:

1. After 30 days of activation.

2. Partially used licences.

  • If a customer has used the software for any part of a term, month, or year, no refund is available for that used period.

3. Downgrade from higher tier.

  • If a customer chose a Lifetime licence but paid only part of the fee, and the licence was converted to a lower tier, no refund is granted for the difference.

4. Breach of Terms.

  • If the licence was terminated because the customer breached the Terms of Service or the Customer Agreement, no refund is granted.

5. Illegal use.

  • If the customer used SkulCBT for illegal purposes, no refund is granted.

6. Fraud.

  • If the customer obtained the licence through fraud or misrepresentation, no refund is granted.

7. Multi-campus without licence.

  • If the customer installed SkulCBT at multiple school locations without paying for additional licences, no refund is granted.

8. Non-use.

  • If the customer did not use the software for the period, that is not grounds for a refund. The licence was available; whether it was used or not is the customer's choice.

9. Failure to complete CBT exams.

  • If a school failed to run their CBT exams during the term, that is not grounds for a refund.

10. Failure of internet or electricity.

  • SkulCBT works offline. If the customer's internet or electricity failed, that is not grounds for a refund.

11. Customer hardware or software issues.

  • If the customer's computer did not meet the minimum requirements, that is not grounds for a refund.

## 1.6 Refunds on Conversion of Higher Tier

If a Customer paid part of the fee for a higher-tier licence (e.g., Lifetime) and the licence was converted to a lower tier under Clause 1.6 of the Customer Agreement:

  • No refund is granted for the difference between the higher tier and the lower tier.
  • The amount paid is credited toward the lower-tier licence.
  • Any excess credit is applied to future renewals.

## 1.7 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, the Customer must:

1. Submit a written request.

Send an email to: emmanueladekunlep@gmail.com

Include in your request:

  • School name.
  • Proprietor name.
  • Licence key.
  • Date of purchase.
  • Amount paid.
  • Reason for the refund request.
  • Bank account details for the refund.

2. Wait for acknowledgement.

The Company will acknowledge your request within three (3) working days.

3. Provide any additional information.

The Company may request additional information to process the request.

4. Wait for the decision.

The Company will respond in writing within fourteen (14) working days of receiving the request.

5. Receive the refund.

If approved, the refund will be processed within thirty (30) days of the decision. Refunds are made by bank transfer to the account provided.

## 1.8 Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed within thirty (30) days of approval.

Refunds are made by bank transfer.

The Company is not responsible for delays caused by:

  • Incorrect bank details provided by the Customer.
  • Bank processing delays.
  • Public holidays.
  • Force majeure events.

## 1.9 Deductions

Refund amounts may be reduced by:

1. Bank charges.

Any bank charges incurred by the Company in receiving or processing the payment may be deducted.

2. Payment gateway fees.

Where payment was made through a payment gateway, the fee charged by the gateway may be deducted.

3. Outstanding amounts.

Any outstanding amounts owed by the Customer to the Company may be deducted from the refund.

4. Damage caused by the Customer.

If the Customer damaged Company property (e.g., hardware, equipment), the cost may be deducted.

## 1.10 Chargebacks

If a Customer initiates a chargeback or payment reversal through their bank or payment provider without first requesting a refund through this Policy, the Company reserves the right to:

  • Suspend the licence immediately.
  • Dispute the chargeback with evidence.
  • Terminate the licence without refund.
  • Pursue legal action to recover the amount.

## 1.11 Refund on Failed Payment

If a payment failed but was deducted from the Customer's account:

  • The Customer should contact their bank first.
  • If the bank confirms the payment was received by the Company, contact us for a refund.
  • The Company will investigate within 14 days.
  • If the payment was indeed received, a full refund will be processed.

## 1.12 Statutory Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy affects the Customer's statutory rights under the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018 (FCCPA).

If the software does not work as agreed, and the Company cannot fix it, the Customer may have a right to a refund under the FCCPA, regardless of the windows in this Policy.
