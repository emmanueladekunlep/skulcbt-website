Why Nigerian Schools Need Laboratory Management

1. Equipment Gets Lost

Without tracking, equipment disappears. Microscopes, chemicals, glassware — all expensive. Laboratory management stops equipment loss.

2. Equipment Breaks Unnoticed

Students break equipment and don't report it. Labs become unsafe. Laboratory management tracks equipment condition and flags issues.

3. No Accountability

Teachers don't know who used equipment. Students don't take responsibility. Laboratory management creates accountability.

4. Wasted Resources

Schools buy equipment they already have because they don't know what's available. Laboratory management saves money.

5. Lab Safety

Broken equipment is dangerous. Laboratory management ensures equipment is maintained and unsafe items are flagged.

How to Use SkulCBT Laboratory Management

Add Labs

Go to Laboratories page. Click Add Lab. Enter name, location, teacher in charge, capacity, and status.

2

Add Equipment

In each lab, click Add Equipment. Enter name, quantity, and condition (Good, Fair, Poor, Broken).

3

4

Monitor Condition

View equipment status at a glance. Update condition as equipment is maintained or repaired.

Equipment Status Guide

  • 🟢 Good: Equipment is in excellent condition. Fully functional.
  • 🟡 Fair: Equipment works but may need minor maintenance soon.
  • 🟠 Poor: Equipment is functional but needs repair. Should be replaced soon.
  • 🔴 Broken: Equipment is not functional. Needs immediate repair or replacement.

FAQ — School Lab Technicians Ask

What is laboratory management software?

Laboratory management software helps schools track lab equipment, manage usage logs, monitor equipment condition, and maintain inventory. It prevents equipment loss and ensures lab safety.

Can I track equipment condition?

Yes. SkulCBT tracks equipment condition as Good, Fair, Poor, or Broken. You can update equipment status and see visual indicators for each item.

Can I log equipment usage?

Yes. You can log who used equipment, when, and what action was taken (Used, Maintained, Checked, Reported Issue). This creates a complete usage history.

Does it work offline?

Yes. SkulCBT is 100% offline-first. You can manage your labs without internet. Perfect for schools in areas with unreliable network.
