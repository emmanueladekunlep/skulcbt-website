What Can the AI Assistant Do?

1. Student Results

  • "Show me SS1 results." — AI shows all SS1 results
  • "Who is the best student in JSS3?" — AI identifies the top student
  • "What is the average score in Mathematics?" — AI calculates the average

2. Fees & Payments

  • "Who owes fees?" — AI lists all students with outstanding balances
  • "How much fees has been collected this term?" — AI shows total collected
  • "Show me Adebayo's fee balance." — AI shows specific student balance

3. Attendance

  • "How many students were absent yesterday?" — AI shows attendance data
  • "Show me attendance for SS1 A." — AI shows class attendance

4. Staff Information

  • "Who are the senior teachers?" — AI lists senior teachers
  • "How many staff do we have?" — AI shows staff count
  • "Show me Mr. Okoro's details." — AI shows staff profile
