Student Promotion - Automate Class Transitions in Nigerian Schools

End of term class transitions used to take hours. Now move all students to the next class with one click. No manual data entry. No errors.

It's the end of term at any Nigerian school. The report cards are signed. The scores are final. Now comes the headache of promotion.

Moving 200+ students from JSS1 to JSS2. Updating class lists. Creating new registers. Updating fees. It's a multi-day nightmare of manual data entry.

💡 The Problem:

Nigerian schools spend an average of 8-12 hours per term manually promoting students to the next class. Errors are common. Students are misplaced. Registers are wrong.

Why Manual Promotion Fails

1. Data Entry Errors

You copy student names from one register to another. Names are misspelled. Admission numbers are wrong. Students are placed in the wrong class arm.

2. It Takes Forever

For a school with 200+ students, manual promotion takes hours. You need to check every student, confirm their class, update records, and create new registers.

3. Records Get Lost

Student academic history doesn't always carry forward. Transcripts are incomplete. Result history is fragmented across terms and classes.

4. Fees Are Not Updated

When students move to a new class, fees change. Manual promotion often misses this. Parents are overcharged or undercharged. Complaints follow.

How SkulCBT Automates Student Promotion

SkulCBT's Student Promotion feature handles everything in 3 simple steps:

Step 1: Select Current Class

Choose the class you want to promote. Example: JSS1. The system shows you all students in that class.

Step 2: Select Target Class

Choose the class students should move to. Example: JSS2. You can also choose class arms (A, B, Science, Art).

Step 3: Promote

Select all students or specific students. Click "Promote". In one click, all students are moved to the new class.

FAQ — Nigerian School Administrators Ask

Can I promote specific students only?

Yes. Select individual students using checkboxes. Only promoted students move to the new class.

What happens to students not promoted?

They remain in their current class. You can promote them later or take other action.

Does promotion work offline?

Yes. SkulCBT works 100% offline. You can promote students without internet.

Can I promote students across different sections?

Yes. From Nursery to Primary, Primary to Secondary. Any class to any class.
