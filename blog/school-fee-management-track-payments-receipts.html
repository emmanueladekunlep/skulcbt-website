Why Nigerian Schools Struggle with Fee Management

1. Excel Spreadsheets Fail

Excel is great for small data. But with 500+ students and multiple fee types, Excel becomes chaos. Formulas break. Parents claim they paid but you can't find the record.

2. No Auto-Balance Tracking

Manual systems don't track previous balance automatically. You have to calculate it each time. Mistakes happen. Parents get wrong balances.

3. Parents Don't Know What They Owe

Parents don't know their balance until they get to the school. This causes frustration and delays payments.

4. Receipts Are Unprofessional

Handwritten receipts look unprofessional. Parents lose them. There's no digital record.

5. No Bulk Communication

Sending fee reminders to 200 parents manually is impossible. Schools rely on word-of-mouth or notices on the gate.

How SkulCBT Solves These Problems

  • Auto-Balance Tracking: Previous balance + new balance calculated automatically. No manual math.
  • Professional PDF Receipts: School logo, clear breakdown, professional design. Print or email.
  • Parent Portal: Parents check balances anytime, anywhere. No excuses.
  • Bulk WhatsApp & Email: Send fee reminders to entire classes in seconds.
  • Debtors List: See who owes what at a glance. Prioritize follow-up.
  • Scholarship & Discount Management: Apply discounts to individual students.

How does SkulCBT fee management work?
