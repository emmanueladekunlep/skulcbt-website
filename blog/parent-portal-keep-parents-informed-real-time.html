Why Nigerian Schools Need a Parent Portal

1. Reduce Parent Complaints

Parents complain when they don't have information. The Parent Portal gives them 24/7 access. No more "I didn't know" complaints.

2. Increase Trust

Transparency builds trust. When parents see everything, they trust the school more. They feel involved in their child's education.

3. Improve Fee Collection

Parents can see their fee balance anytime. No more "I didn't know I owed." Fee collection improves significantly.

4. Save Staff Time

School staff spend hours answering parent questions. The Parent Portal answers questions automatically. Staff focus on teaching.

Engaged parents are more supportive. They help their children at home. They attend school events. They become partners in education.

FAQ — School Administrators Ask

What is a parent portal?

A parent portal is a secure online platform where parents can access their child's school information including report cards, results, attendance, fee balances, and school announcements.

Can parents see multiple children in one login?

Yes. Parents with multiple children in the school can see all their children in one dashboard. They can switch between children easily.

Do parents need an account to use the portal?

Yes. Parents log in with their phone number and password (default: parent123). Accounts are created automatically when students are added.

Can parents see fee balances?

Yes. Parents can see total fees, amount paid, outstanding balance, and payment history for all their children.
