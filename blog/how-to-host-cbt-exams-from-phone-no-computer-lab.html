How Phone-Based CBT Exam Hosting Works

1

Create Your Exam

Add exam title, category (WAEC, JAMB, NECO, etc.), subject, class, and duration. Add questions manually or import from Word/PDF/TXT.

2

Import Questions (One Click)

Upload from Word, PDF, or TXT. SkulCBT auto-detects multiple choice, true/false, and theory questions. No manual formatting needed.

3

Open Exam Host on Your Phone

Open the SkulCBT Exam Host page on your phone. Select the exam and class. Choose assessment type (Test, Exam, Quiz).

4

Students Join via QR Code

Students scan the QR code from your phone screen. Or they enter the session code manually. No account needed. No registration.

5

Live Progress & Monitoring

Watch who has logged in, who is taking the exam, who has completed. See live progress on your phone.

6

Auto-Grading & Instant Results

When students submit, results are auto-graded instantly. Students see their score, percentage, and pass/fail status immediately.

7

Sync to Server (Offline)

No internet needed during the exam. Later, reconnect to school WiFi and sync results to the main server. All data saved locally.

1. No Computer Lab Required

2. Students Use Their Own Devices

Students join on phones, tablets, or laptops. They already have these devices. No need to buy expensive computers.

3. Works 100% Offline

Internet is unreliable in many parts of Nigeria. SkulCBT works 100% offline. No internet needed for the exam. No data costs.

5. Auto-Grading Saves Time

6. WAEC/NECO/JAMB Ready

Students practice with WAEC, NECO, and JAMB style questions. They get familiar with CBT format before the real exams.

7. Secure & Cheating-Proof

How to Import Questions from Word/PDF/TXT

Creating questions manually takes time. SkulCBT has one-click question import:

  • Word (.docx): Upload your document. SkulCBT auto-detects questions and options.
  • PDF (.pdf): Upload your PDF. SkulCBT extracts questions automatically.
  • TXT (.txt): Upload text files. SkulCBT parses the structure.
  • Auto-Detects: Multiple choice, true/false, and theory questions are detected automatically.
  • Bulk Upload: Import hundreds of questions in seconds.

  • Theory: Auto-detects theory questions with marking scheme.
