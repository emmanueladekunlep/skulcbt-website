FAQ — School Administrators Ask

What does the school analytics dashboard show?

The analytics dashboard shows key metrics including total students, staff, classes, fees collected, collection rate, average score, top performing classes, and champions for rewards and scholarships.

How does the champions and scholarships system work?

SkulCBT identifies Term Champions (per term), Two-Term Champions (1st + 2nd term combined), and Full Session Champions (scholarship eligible). This helps schools reward academic excellence.

Can I filter analytics by session and term?

Yes. You can filter analytics by session, term, and assessment type to get specific insights for any period.

Does it work offline?

Yes. SkulCBT is 100% offline-first. You can access all analytics without internet. Perfect for schools in areas with unreliable network.
