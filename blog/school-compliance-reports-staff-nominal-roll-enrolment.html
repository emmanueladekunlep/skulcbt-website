1. Staff Nominal Roll

  • Complete list: All staff with names, positions, qualifications, and contact details
  • Teaching vs Non-Teaching: Clear breakdown of staff categories
  • Filter: By session and term
  • Export: Print or PDF ready for submission

2. Student Enrolment Report

  • Total Students: Overall enrolment numbers
  • Gender Breakdown: Male/Female counts and percentages
  • Class-wise Distribution: Students per class with details
  • Age Distribution: Age range of enrolled students
  • Filter: By session and term

3. Infrastructure Report

  • Classrooms: Total classrooms and usage
  • Computers: Number of computers available
  • Facilities: Libraries, labs, and other facilities
  • Student-to-Classroom Ratio: Key metric for compliance

4. Financial Summary

  • Total Fees: Total fees expected for the term/session
  • Fees Collected: Total collected so far
  • Outstanding Balance: Total outstanding fees
  • Collection Rate: Percentage of fees collected

FAQ — School Administrators Ask

What compliance reports does SkulCBT generate?

SkulCBT generates Staff Nominal Roll, Student Enrolment Report, Infrastructure Report, and Financial Summary. All reports can be filtered by session and term.

Can I export compliance reports to PDF?

Yes. All compliance reports can be printed or exported to PDF for submission to government agencies or school records.

What is included in the Student Enrolment Report?

The Student Enrolment Report shows total students, gender breakdown (male/female), class-wise distribution, and age distribution for the selected session and term.

Yes. SkulCBT is 100% offline-first. You can generate all compliance reports without internet. Perfect for schools in areas with unreliable network.
