FAQ — Nigerian Schools Ask

What is a student transcript?

A student transcript is a complete academic history showing all subjects, scores, grades, and remarks for every term and session. It provides a comprehensive view of a student's academic journey.

Can I export a student transcript to PDF?

Yes. SkulCBT allows you to export any student's transcript as a professional PDF file for printing or sharing with parents, universities, or employers.

Does the transcript show cumulative performance?

Yes. The transcript shows per subject scores with grades, term averages, and cumulative performance across all terms and sessions. You can see the student's progress over time.

Can I click on a score to see exam answers?

Yes. Click any score in the transcript to see the student's exam answers. This is great for detailed performance reviews.
