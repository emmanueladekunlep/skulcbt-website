Step-by-Step Guide to Generating Your Clash-Free Timetable

Step 1: School Setup (Done Once)

Before generating your first timetable, you need to set up your school. This is a one-time setup.

  • School Config: Set days per week, periods per day, lunch break, break periods, and period times.
  • Friday Config: Enable special Friday schedule with custom periods.
  • Classes: Add all your classes (e.g., JSS1, JSS2, SS1, SS2, SS3) with arms (A, B, Science, Art).
  • Subjects: Add all subjects with codes (e.g., MAT, ENG, CHEM, PHY).
  • Teachers: Add all teachers with their subjects and availability.
  • Rooms: Add classrooms, labs, halls with capacity.

📝 One-Time Setup:

This setup takes about 30-60 minutes. Once done, you can generate timetables forever with one click.

Step 2: Configure Constraints

Set up your specific constraints:

  • Teacher Availability: Set which days and periods each teacher is available (e.g., Mr. Okoro: Mon, Wed, Fri only).
  • Double Periods: Mark practical subjects (Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Technical Drawing) as "Double Period Required."
  • Elective Blocks: Group elective subjects together.
  • Special Programs: Add assembly, sports, worship to specific days and periods.
  • Fixed Slots: Lock specific periods for specific subjects/teachers.
  • Global Constraints: Set teacher max periods/day, max consecutive periods, no back-to-back same subject.

Step 3: Generate the Timetable

Now for the magic:

  • Select a class or "All Classes."
  • Click "Generate."
  • Wait 1.4 seconds.
  • Your clash-free timetable appears.

What Makes a Timetable "Clash-Free"?

A clash-free timetable means no conflicts:

  • No Teacher Clash: A teacher cannot be in two places at once.
  • No Room Clash: A room cannot be used by two classes simultaneously.
  • No Subject Clash: A class cannot have two subjects at the same time.
  • No Overload: Teachers have breathing space. No 6 consecutive periods.
  • Double Periods Respected: Practical subjects are paired correctly.
  • Availability Respected: Part-time teachers' schedules are honored.

Advanced Features of the AI Timetable Generator

  • Section Filter: Filter by school section (Nursery, Lower Primary, Upper Primary, JSS, SSS).
  • Merged Classes: Combine classes where needed (e.g., SS1 Science and SS1 Art for general subjects).
  • Elective Optimization: Group electives efficiently for student choice.
  • Teacher Workload Balance: Distribute periods fairly across all teachers.
  • Room Optimization: Maximize room usage while avoiding conflicts.
  • Breathing Space: Ensure teachers have adequate breaks.
