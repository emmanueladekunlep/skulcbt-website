FAQ — Nigerian Teachers Ask

What is the Champion of the Week feature?

The Champion of the Week identifies the best performing student each week based on Weekly Assessment scores. It celebrates academic excellence and motivates students to perform better.

How does the Weekly Assessment work?

Teachers enter scores for each student per subject per week (1-12). The system automatically identifies top performers, best/worst subjects, class averages, and the Champion of the Week.

Can I see weekly assessment analytics?

Yes. The analytics dashboard shows top 3 students with medals, best and poorest performing subjects per class, class average, and assessment count.

Is Weekly Assessment only for primary schools?

Weekly Assessment is designed for primary schools but can be used for any class. It's especially effective for early years where weekly tracking is important.
