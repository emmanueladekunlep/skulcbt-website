About SkulCBT

Built by Nigerians, for Nigerians. Our mission is to democratize access to quality education technology for every school in Nigeria.

Our Story

From Vision to Reality

SkulCBT was founded by Emmanuel Adekunle Peace with a simple but powerful vision: to build education technology that actually works for Nigerian schools.

After years of watching Nigerian schools struggle with expensive, internet-dependent software that didn't understand local needs, Emmanuel decided to build something different.

The result is SkulCBT — a complete offline-first school management and CBT platform that works without internet, can be hosted from a phone, and is affordable for every Nigerian school.

Today, SkulCBT serves schools across Nigeria — from single-campus schools to multi-campus groups — and we're just getting started.

Why Nigeria?

Built for Nigerian Schools

We understand the unique challenges of the Nigerian education system:

  • ✓Unreliable internet connectivity in many areas
  • ✓Limited access to computer labs and hardware
  • ✓Budget constraints for expensive software subscriptions
  • ✓Need for WAEC/NECO/JAMB-compliant systems
  • ✓Demand for locally-built solutions that understand our context
