For Schools — Getting Started

What is SkulCBT?

SkulCBT is Nigeria's #1 offline-first school management platform. It handles everything a school needs — students, staff, classes, subjects, fees, results, report cards, CBT exams, attendance, parent portals, and government compliance reports. It runs on your own Windows computer and works fully without internet.

Will it work on my computer?

Yes. SkulCBT runs on any Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer with at least 4 GB RAM. Old laptops, new laptops, desktops — all work. No special hardware needed.

Do I need internet to use SkulCBT?

No. SkulCBT works fully offline. You can add students, enter results, print report cards, collect fees, run CBT exams, and manage everything without any internet connection. Internet is only used silently for cloud backup, the parent portal, and automatic updates. If internet is down, the app keeps working exactly the same.

What happens when NEPA takes light?

Nothing bad. The app is on your computer, not on the internet. A power outage only affects the computer itself — same as it affects Microsoft Word. When power returns, open SkulCBT and continue from where you stopped. With an inverter or generator for your computer, you can keep using SkulCBT throughout.

For Schools — More

How long does installation take?

About two minutes. Download the installer, double-click it, follow the three steps on screen. The app opens by itself when done. And you will never have to reinstall again — every future update installs silently on its own.

How do I activate my license?

Open SkulCBT → click Activate → enter the license key your agent or SkulCBT support gave you. Type your school name exactly as it appears on the license. Choose your package (Essential, Professional, or Enterprise) and the plan (Termly, Yearly, or Lifetime). Click Activate. Done.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. 14 days, full access to all features. No card, no payment. When the trial ends, activate your license to keep using the app. Your data stays on the computer — nothing is lost.

Can I add all my students quickly?

Yes. Two ways: one at a time through the app, or bulk import from a CSV file. You can add 1,000 students in one import. The importer shows you exactly which rows succeeded and which failed, with a clear reason for each failure.

Does SkulCBT work on phone or tablet?

The main admin app runs on Windows computers. But parents, directors, and students access their own portals from any phone, tablet, or computer — no app to install, just a web link.

Can I use SkulCBT for multiple campuses?

Yes. SkulCBT Enterprise supports multiple schools or campuses in one installation. Each campus has its own data, and you see everything from one dashboard.

Fees & Payments

How do I set school fees?

Fees → set fees per class or per student. Create fee types — Tuition, PTA, Uniform, Books, Bus, etc. — with any amount you choose. Set them per term, per session, or however your school charges.

Can I track who has paid and who hasn't?

Yes. Every fee record shows the amount billed, amount paid, and balance. Filter to see all defaulters by class, and print or export the list. This is the single most popular feature for school owners.

Can I print receipts?

Yes. Every payment generates a receipt with your school logo, the student's name, the amount, and a receipt number. Print it or save as PDF.

Can I add discounts or scholarships?

Yes. Add a discount per student or per fee — percentage or fixed amount. The discount shows clearly on the receipt and the report.

Can I carry forward previous balances?

Yes. When a student has an unpaid balance from a previous term, SkulCBT shows it alongside the new fees so you always see the total amount owed.

Results & Report Cards

How do I enter results?

Results → choose class, subject, term → enter scores for each student. SkulCBT supports CA1, CA2, CA3, CA4, and Exam scoring — or you can customise the assessment structure to match your school.

Can I print report cards automatically?

Yes. Report Card → choose class → Print All. SkulCBT generates every student's report card with results, comments, grades, class average, position in class, and attendance — all calculated automatically.

Can I include the school logo and principal's signature?

Yes. Upload your logo and the principal's signature once in School Profile. Every report card uses them automatically.

How are comments and grades handled?

Comments can be typed per student or generated by the built-in AI assistant. Grades are calculated automatically using your own grading system — not a fixed one. Configure your grade boundaries once, SkulCBT applies them everywhere.

How is class position calculated?

Automatically. SkulCBT ranks each student against the class for the term based on total score. No manual work.

Can I generate a transcript for a former student?

Yes. Transcript → choose student → choose sessions → generate. SkulCBT combines all their academic history into one document.

CBT Exams

Can I run CBT exams without a computer lab?

Yes — this is one of the most powerful features of SkulCBT. You host the exam on the teacher's own computer or phone. Students join using their own phones, tablets, or any device connected to the school WiFi. No computer lab needed.

How do students access the exam?

You open the Exam Host page on the teacher's computer. SkulCBT generates a QR code and a short session code. Students scan the QR code or enter the session code on their phone — they are in. They type their admission number to log in.

Does CBT work without internet?

Yes. Once the exam is hosted on the school's own WiFi (no internet required), students connect directly to the teacher's computer. Even if the internet is completely down, the exam runs perfectly.

What if students don't have phones?

Any device with a browser works — phones, tablets, old laptops, a school desktop. If you have a few devices and many students, they can take turns or take the exam in groups.

Can I import exam questions from Word or PDF?

Yes. Type or copy your questions into a Word document with a clear format, upload it, and SkulCBT extracts the questions, options, and correct answers automatically.

Can students submit before the last question?

Yes. The Submit button is always visible. A student can submit at any time — after the first question, midway, or at the end. They don't have to reach the last question to finish.

What if the time runs out?

The exam submits automatically when the timer hits zero. Nothing the student answered is lost.

Can I use SkulCBT for WAEC, NECO, or JAMB practice?

Yes. You can create unlimited CBT practice exams for any subject, class, or level. Many schools use SkulCBT for weekly mock exams and WAEC/JAMB preparation.

How does SkulCBT prevent exam malpractice?

Each student logs in with their own admission number, the exam is timed, and only one submission is allowed per student. You can also require a specific session code per exam — nobody outside the classroom can join.

Parent Portal & Director Access

What is the parent portal?

A website where parents log in from their phone to see their child's results, fees, attendance, and report cards — any time, anywhere. No phone calls to the school, no visits, no waiting.

How do parents get access?

Each parent uses their phone number (the one you already have on file) and a 4-digit PIN they create on their first login. Nothing to download. No app to install. Just a web link.

What can parents see?

Results per term, report cards with the school's own branding, fee balance and payment history, attendance summary, and any newsletter the school publishes.

Is it safe? Can other parents see my students' data?

No. Each parent sees only their own children. The system isolates every school and every parent strictly. A parent from one school cannot see any data from another school, and a parent in your school cannot see another family's child.

What if a parent forgets their PIN?

The director can reset it from the Director Dashboard. The parent then creates a new PIN on their next login.

Is the parent portal mandatory?

No. It's optional. You can choose not to share the link with parents, and the app works exactly the same without it.

Can I check my school from anywhere as the owner?

Yes. The Director Dashboard is a separate login on the same parent portal website. Log in with your phone number and PIN from any phone, tablet, or computer and see live numbers: total students, total fees billed, total collected, total outstanding, students per class, and the top 50 fee defaulters. Read-only — you can't accidentally change data.

Data, Backup & Safety

Where is my school's data stored?

On your own computer, in a secure local database. It stays on your machine always. A backup copy is also synced to our secure cloud servers so you never lose it if the computer breaks.

What if my computer spoils?

Your data is safe. Install SkulCBT on a new computer, activate it with the same license, and your school data is restored from the cloud backup. Nothing is lost.

Is my data backed up automatically?

Yes. SkulCBT syncs your data to our secure cloud in the background. You don't need to click anything. You can also create local backups anytime from Settings → Backup.

Can someone hack into my school's data?

Your data lives on your own computer, not on the public internet. Cloud backups are encrypted. The parent portal shows data only to the parent who owns it — verified by phone number and PIN.

What happens if I uninstall SkulCBT?

Your data is preserved. SkulCBT stores school data in a separate folder that is never deleted by uninstall. When you reinstall, your data is still there.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export students, staff, results, fees, and compliance reports to Excel at any time.

Do you see my school's data?

No. Your school's data is your own. We don't look at your records, we don't share them with anyone, and we don't use them for anything other than storing your backup so you don't lose them.

Updates & Future Versions

How do updates work?

SkulCBT updates itself silently. When we release a new version, your computer checks for it in the background. When the computer is idle, the update installs on its own — no clicking, no prompts, no reinstall.

Do I have to reinstall to get new features?

No. This is the last thing you ever need to worry about. Every future improvement reaches you automatically. You never reinstall SkulCBT again.

What if I'm offline when an update comes?

The update simply waits. The next time your computer is online, it downloads and installs in the background. Nothing for you to do.

Can I check for updates myself?

Yes. Settings → Updates → Check for Updates. If a new version exists, SkulCBT will tell you. It will still install silently within 24 hours even if you don't check.

Will an update ever break my data?

No. Updates replace the app's code, never your data. Your students, results, fees, and settings are untouched.

For Agents

How do I become a SkulCBT agent?

Visit the agent page, fill the registration form with your name, email, phone, and password. You get a referral code immediately and can start selling right away.

Is registration free?

Registration is ₦10,000. As a launch appreciation, we are waiving it for the first 50 agents — sign up early to get yours free. After the first 50, the standard ₦10,000 registration fee applies.

How much do I earn per sale?

35% of every sale. Sell a ₦150,000 Professional Lifetime license — you earn ₦52,500.

When do I get paid?

Commissions are credited to your SkulCBT agent balance the moment the sale is approved by admin. Payouts run on the 1st of every month for withdrawal requests made before the last day of the previous month.

How do I withdraw?

Agent Dashboard → Withdraw → enter amount, bank name, account number, account name. Requests are reviewed and paid on the next payout day. Minimum withdrawal is ₦10,000.

How does the downline work?

When you recruit another agent, they become your Level 1 downline. When they sell, you earn 5% of the sale price. When they recruit another agent (your Level 2), you earn 3% of that agent's sales. And Level 3 earns you 1%.

What's the recruitment bonus?

₦5,000 per active recruit. When a downline agent makes their first sale, you get ₦5,000 credited to your balance.

What are the agent ranks?

Bronze (3 sales), Silver (10 sales), Gold (25 sales), Platinum (50 sales), Diamond (100 sales + recruitment team). Each rank unlocks a higher share of the profit pool. Promotions are automatic when you hit the threshold.

What's my pitch to schools?

Lead with pain, not features. Most Nigerian schools struggle with three things: fee collection, report card printing, and parent communication. SkulCBT fixes all three in one package. Show, don't tell — open the app on your laptop and let them see a report card print in 10 seconds.

What if a school says it's too expensive?

Show the math. A school with 200 students on the yearly plan = ₦333 per student for the whole year. Compare that to the hours and errors saved on report cards alone. Also mention the Lifetime option — pay once, use forever.

What if they ask how it works without internet?

This is your strongest selling point. Say: 'It works even when NEPA takes light and even when your MTN data finishes. It's on your computer. Internet is only for backup.'
