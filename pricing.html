Common Questions

Pricing Questions

What counts as a term?+

A term is one of the three academic terms in the Nigerian school calendar (First, Second, Third). Each term you renew or, if you chose yearly or lifetime, you don't pay again until the next cycle.

Do I need a separate payment for each school branch?+

Each school or branch needs its own license. For multi-campus schools, contact us for a group discount on the Enterprise plan.

Is there a free trial?+

Yes. The free trial unlocks full Enterprise features for a limited period so you can test everything before paying. Download the installer to start.

How do I pay?+

Bank transfer to the account we share after your demo. You receive a license key by WhatsApp or email once payment clears. Contact us to start.

Do updates cost extra?+

No. Every plan includes lifetime free updates. Updates install automatically — no action needed from your school.
