Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SkulCBT pricing

Is this a subscription or one-time payment?

One-time payment per term. No monthly or annual subscriptions. You pay once and use SkulCBT for the entire term with lifetime updates.

What is the pricing for SkulCBT?

SkulCBT offers three plans: Essential at ₦20,000/term, Professional at ₦40,000/term, and Enterprise at ₦75,000/term. All plans are one-time payment with lifetime updates.

Can I upgrade from ESS to PRO later?

Yes! You can upgrade anytime. You only pay the difference between the plans. Contact our sales team for a seamless upgrade.

What support is included?

All plans include email and WhatsApp support. PRO and ENT plans include priority support and 1-on-1 onboarding.

Is there a free trial?

Yes! SkulCBT offers a 14-day free trial for all plans. Request your demo here.

Can I get a discount for multiple terms?

Yes! We offer significant discounts for yearly and lifetime licenses. Contact our sales team for a custom quote.
