FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SkulCBT's features and capabilities.

What is the AI Timetable Generator in SkulCBT? +

SkulCBT's AI Timetable Generator automatically creates clash-free timetables for Nigerian schools. It respects teacher availability, subject periods per week, class constraints, and generates optimized timetables in minutes instead of hours of manual work.

How does the Weekly Assessment and Best Student feature work? +

SkulCBT's Weekly Assessment lets teachers track student performance weekly. The system automatically identifies the Best Student of the Week, Champion of the Week, top 3 students per class, best performing subject, and weakest performing subject with detailed analytics.

Can I host CBT exams from my phone with SkulCBT? +

Yes. SkulCBT allows teachers to host CBT exams directly from their phone. Students join using any device with a browser. No computer lab is needed. Works 100% offline with auto-grading and instant results.

Does SkulCBT work offline? +

Yes. SkulCBT is 100% offline-first. All features work without any internet connection. Data stays on your school's local network and syncs to the cloud when internet returns.

What is the pricing for SkulCBT? +

SkulCBT offers three plans: Essential at ₦20,000 per term, Professional at ₦40,000 per term, and Enterprise at ₦75,000 per term. Yearly and lifetime options are also available. See full pricing.

Does SkulCBT generate report cards automatically? +

Yes. SkulCBT generates professional report cards automatically with teacher and principal comments. It includes psychomotor skills assessment and can be shared with parents through the portal.

What is the Student Transcript feature? +

SkulCBT's Student Transcript provides a complete academic history across all terms and sessions. It shows per-subject scores with grades, term averages, cumulative performance, and can be exported as PDF.
